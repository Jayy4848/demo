Key Innovations That Set N-MER Apart

Proven in the field. Protected by 7 patents. Designed to do what others can't.

🛡️

Zone-0 Certified Safety

Asia's first Zone-0 certified robot operates safely in continuous explosive atmosphere. Intrinsically safe within live tanks containing highly volatile substances without risk of sparking an explosion.

⚙️

Advanced Sludge Cutting

Robust front-mounted auger physically breaks down solidified deposits and cake-like sludge. Unlike vacuum systems that clog easily, our mechanism handles dense sludge that typically requires manual chipping.

📹

HD Visual Inspection

ATEX-certified cameras with high-intensity LED provide real-time HD video feeds. Operators receive clear visual records revealing corrosion, pitting, or cracks that would otherwise go unnoticed until a leak occurs.

⏱️

Minimal Downtime

Robotic solutions operate with significantly reduced downtime compared to manual cleaning. Reclaim lost capacity and revenue faster while avoiding lengthy shutdowns and complex safety permitting.

RaaS Business Model
